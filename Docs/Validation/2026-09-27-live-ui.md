## Defects and fixes

1. **False tracked drift after naming an untitled level.** `ObjectState` records attach-parent and child paths that include the world package. The editor's Save As also saves the dirty manifest, and UE's `GRedirectCollector` asset-path redirection rewrites the manifest's `FSoftObjectPath` fields while leaving tracked-state `FString`s on `/Temp/Untitled_1.Untitled:`. `WorldPath` then resolves, so `SaveImportedResult` skipped its rebind branch and every later check reported three false differences. Fix: `ConVerseImportProcessing::RebaseTemporaryWorld` maps `/Temp/…:PersistentLevel.` references onto the manifest's permanent world, only when the recorded session owner resolves in that world with the recorded GUID and session tag. `CheckState` compares through it, and explicit save persists it. The contract and ADR 0006 carry the clarification.
2. **Session restore hid the effective source.** The source `SEditableTextBox` had no initial `.Text`, unlike the destination, and status was never recomputed after restore. Fix: initialize the box from `SourcePath` and call `MarkInputsChanged` at the end of `Construct`, with a "Restored the last executed settings" status when inputs are valid.

| Test | What it proves |
|---|---|
| `DatasmithHISM.Persistence.UnnamedMapFirstSaveStaysVerified` | Imports into an untitled level. | `DatasmithHISM.OptimizedImport.PanelSessionRestoreShowsInputs` | Writes a known `Session.json` (restoring the user's file afterwards), constructs the panel, and requires the visible source/destination to equal the effective inputs, Ready status, no "select a source" text, and no asset changes. |
